🌾 How to Play / कैसे खेलें
Farm → Process → Sell → Expand
🕹️
Move: drag anywhere on screen (or arrow / WASD keys) to walk your farmer.
🌾
Harvest: walk through golden ripe crops — they cut straight into your bag 🎒.
🐔
Process: carry crops to the animals & sheds — Coop makes eggs, Bhains makes milk, Mithai shop makes sweets. Stand next to them to drop inputs and pick up the finished goods.
🚚
Sell: stand at the मंडी truck to sell your whole bag at the live market price.
📋
Orders: fill named customers' orders for bonus ₹ and XP. Tap the orders tab to hide / show the board.
🏍️
Doodhwala: the milkman drives by and buys milk at a premium ₹70.
🗺️
Expand: open the map drawer or walk onto glowing pads to buy land, bigger machines, workers & storage.
☀️
Weather: rain grows crops faster but lowers prices; sun = high prices but crows raid crops — buy a scarecrow.
🔊
Sound: tap the speaker button (top-right) to toggle cow / hen sounds & effects on or off.
